Look beyond the thumbnail

Fit and QC checklist

  • Clothing: compare chest width, body length, shoulders and sleeves against a garment that fits you.
  • Shoes: confirm the sizing system, width guidance and whether a measurement describes the foot or insole.
  • Photos: distinguish listing images from photos of the exact item allocated to your order.
  • Condition: examine visible stitching, closures, marks and whether the received color matches your selected option.
  • Missing details: request measurements or additional photos before approving shipment, where the service permits.
  • Evidence limits: photographs alone do not verify authenticity, composition, durability or a guaranteed fit.
Before you continue

KakoBuy spreadsheet FAQ

Is this an official KakoBuy spreadsheet?

No. This is an independent discovery page using a sourced CAFANS catalog snapshot. It is not operated by KakoBuy and does not process orders or payments.

Where do the product links and buttons go?

Product cards open their named CAFANS listings. The “KakoBuy spreadsheet” and “Buy it now” buttons both open https://cafans.com. Check the available purchasing options on CAFANS; the buttons do not place an order.

Is the directory live or automatically synchronized?

This page contains eight products imported from the CAFANS homepage on 19 November 2026. It is a static snapshot, not a live stock feed. Search and filters work on those eight products; visit CAFANS for the current catalog.

Do the displayed prices include shipping?

No. These are the estimated USD item prices displayed by the source. Confirm the selected variant, current price, domestic delivery, agent or payment fees, international shipping and any applicable additional charges separately.

Does every product have verified QC photos?

No such verification is claimed here. The cards show images from the source listings. If QC photos are available through your selected service, confirm which item or order they show before using them to make a decision.

How should I choose between two similar finds?

Compare the exact variant, seller details, quantities, measurements, stated materials, available photo evidence and the complete quoted cost. Keep unresolved questions visible instead of assuming two similar thumbnails describe the same product.

Do I need an account to browse?

No account is needed to browse, search or filter this page. Account and checkout requirements depend on the destination website and purchasing service you choose.
